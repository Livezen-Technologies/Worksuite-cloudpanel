# App icons

Logos of the applications in the one-click catalogue, used only to identify each
application. Each logo belongs to its project.

- Most come from Coolify's service templates (`public/svgs`, Apache-2.0):
  https://github.com/coollabsio/coolify
- The rest are from Simple Icons (CC0-1.0), coloured with each brand's own colour:
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

PNGs are scaled down to 128 px; very large SVGs were rasterised to 128 px PNGs.
